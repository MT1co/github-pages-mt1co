About

I come from the non-profit sector, where the data that matters is scattered across a donor CRM, an online giving platform, an email tool, event registrations and a stack of spreadsheets, and the people who need answers rarely have time to stitch them together.

I build the plumbing that brings those sources into one reliable place on Google Cloud, then work with leadership to decide what the numbers mean and what to do next. The goal is never a dashboard for its own sake; it is a clearer answer to “is this working, and where should we invest?”
