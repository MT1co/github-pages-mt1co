README.md

I come from the non-profit sector, where the data that matters is scattered across a donor CRM, an online giving platform, an email tool, event registrations and a stack of spreadsheets, and the people who need answers rarely have time to stitch them together.

I build the plumbing that brings those sources into one reliable place on Google Cloud, then work with leadership to decide what the numbers mean and what to do next. The goal is never a dashboard for its own sake; it is a clearer answer to “is this working, and where should we invest?”

approach

A typical engagement follows the data from source to decision:

# sources                ingest            warehouse            insight
donor CRM        ─┐
online giving    ─┤
email marketing  ─┼──▶  Cloud Run  ──▶  BigQuery  ──▶  Looker Studio
events / tickets ─┤     (scheduled)     raw → clean     dashboards
GA4              ─┘                     → reporting     + strategy
  • Start with the question. Agree on the decisions the data must support before writing any code.
  • Automate the boring parts. No more monthly CSV exports and copy-paste reports.
  • Keep costs non-profit sized. Serverless, pay-per-use Google Cloud services, with spend you can see.
  • Leave it maintainable. Documented, version-controlled and handed over so your team owns it.
