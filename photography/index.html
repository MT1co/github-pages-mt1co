Photography & content

Authentic images and video for your channels.

I found my passion for photography and video when I started my career as a direct marketing fundraiser. As a marketer, I was always starved for authentic, impactful images and video to help tell a story, and I was reluctant to use stock images because they were clearly less meaningful.

So I picked up a camera and got what was needed myself, and I’ve never looked back. The passion carried into outdoor and physical hobbies like hiking, camping, rucking and CrossFit, and eventually grew into a much-needed service for the organizations I was a part of.
